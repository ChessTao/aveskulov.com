About Valeriy

As a chess coach, I work with players who want clearer thinking, stronger practical habits, and a training process connected to their real games. My approach combines analysis, calculation, strategic understanding, opening work, endgame technique, and tournament psychology.

Training method

A calm system for sharper chess decisions.

The method is built around understanding the player first. Openings, calculation, strategy, and tournament psychology are treated as parts of one practical system.

How lessons are built

Every student needs a different balance of analysis, guided practice, independent homework, and tournament support. The aim is not to collect information, but to make better decisions under real game pressure.

Game reviewPattern-finding through recent tournament games.
CalculationCandidate moves, visualization, and decision discipline.
