## Svyatoslav Bazakutsa

- In 2022, after becoming a refugee together with his mother, Svyatoslav Bazakutsa became European Champion U-14.
- He was seeded only 22nd, scored 8/9, and gained 165 Elo points.
- At the 2023 European Individual Chess Championship in Vrnjacka Banja, Serbia, he scored his first IM norm.
- In 2024, he won the Norway Chess Open and achieved his first GM norm.

## Francois Oberholzer

- FM Francois Oberholzer from South Africa improved his South African rating from 1756 to 1954 in less than one year.
- He placed 3rd in the National Championship U-16 in 2010.
- He won the RSA U-18 Championship in 2011.
- He finished second in the 2012 African Youth Championships and became an FM.
- He shared 1st-3rd places there, while officially taking 2nd place U-18.
- In the 2014 South African Open, despite being seeded #23, he placed clear 4th with 8/11, behind one GM, one IM, and one WIM.
- In the 2015 South Africa Open, he had another strong performance, added 88 Elo points, and beat an opponent rated 400 points higher with Black.

## Dr. Jeffrey Plew

- Dr. Jeffrey Plew from Australia started lessons in January 2010.
- He played in three Doerberl Cups and improved each year.
- In the 2010 Doerberl Cup, he scored 50% in the B group.
- In the 2011 Doerberl Cup, he shared 3rd-4th place.
- In the 2012 Doerberl Cup main event, he beat his first master and gained many Elo points.
- At the 2012 World Open in Philadelphia, he achieved a performance rating of about 2200, beat another FM in round 1, and drew a WGM.
- In 2013, he completed a double at the Dubbo Open by winning both the New South Wales Country Championship and the Dubbo Open.
